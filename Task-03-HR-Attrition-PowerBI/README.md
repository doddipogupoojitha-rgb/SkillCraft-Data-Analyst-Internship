# Task 03: HR Employee Attrition - Interactive Visualization

**Objective:** Why are employees leaving?

**Dataset:** HR Employee Attrition (1,470 employees)

**Tools Used:** Power BI / Tableau

**Dashboard Features:**
- Filters: Department, Age Group, Gender, Business Travel
- KPIs: Attrition Rate 16.1%, Avg Age 36, Avg Salary $6,502
- Charts: Attrition by Department, Overtime vs Attrition, Age Group analysis

**Key Insights:**
- Sales Dept highest attrition 20.8%
- Employees with Overtime = 30% attrition vs 10% without
- Age 25-35 group highest leaving
- Single employees attrition more
- Low Job Satisfaction = main reason

**Recommendation:** Reduce overtime, improve work-life balance, focus on Sales dept retention
