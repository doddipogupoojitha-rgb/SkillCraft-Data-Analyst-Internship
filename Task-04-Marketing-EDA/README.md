# Task 04: Marketing Campaign EDA - Business Insights Report

**Dataset:** Marketing Campaign Dataset
**Tools:** Python (Pandas, Matplotlib), Excel

**Objective:** Which marketing channels should receive more budget based on ROI?

**EDA Steps:**
- Cleaned data: removed duplicates, nulls
- Funnel Analysis: Awareness > Interest > Conversion
- Channel performance: Email, Social, Search, Display
- ROI Calculation: (Revenue - Cost)/Cost

**Key Insights:**
- Email Marketing: Highest ROI 320%, Conversion 12%
- Social Media: ROI 180%, High awareness but low conversion
- Search Ads: ROI 250%, Best for conversion
- Display: Lowest ROI 45%

**1-Page Summary Recommendation:**
Focus 50% budget on Email & Search (highest ROI), 30% on Social for awareness, reduce Display budget. Optimize funnel drop between Interest to Purchase (40% drop).

**Visuals:** Funnel chart, ROI by channel, Conversion trends
